# Deposition Outline Builder
*A general AI drafting aid for lawyers. Review the output before use.*

## Paste this into your approved AI assistant

Help me prepare an editable deposition outline from the sources I have authorized you to use. Ask which court or rules govern, what kind of witness will appear, the purpose of the examination, and any notice or order that limits its scope. Do not assume civil rules or terminology carry over from one jurisdiction to another.

1. Make a short table: issue, fact to establish, witness's basis of knowledge, and best source with a pinpoint. Mark uncertain or disputed information.
2. Organize the examination by topic. For each topic, give the objective, short questions a lawyer can read aloud, and any exhibit or follow-up. Keep preparation notes separate from words to be read aloud.
3. Introduce each exhibit only after establishing the relevant foundation. Pin a quotation to the actual page. Include short branches for a denial, lack of memory, or lack of firsthand knowledge. Do not assume the witness saw or acted on something merely because it appears in a document.
4. End with a short list of missing sources, open foundations, and the highest-priority questions if time runs short.
5. Check each pin and quotation against its source. Flag uncertain rule language instead of supplying it as settled.

Prepare a draft only. Do not contact anyone, send exhibits, file, or upload private material to another service. Use an invented example when testing a new AI tool. Ask the lawyer to verify governing rules, limitations, and the final script.
