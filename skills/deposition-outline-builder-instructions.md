# Start Here: Deposition Outline Builder

## What you need

An AI assistant your firm approves for the material you intend to use, the generic prompt from the GitHub listing, and an editable document for your review. No paid plugin, special account, or firm-specific template is required to try it with fictional material.

## Try a five-minute example first

Tell the AI: “I’m testing with fictional facts. The witness worked at a library and recalls a box delivery. The fictional receipt, page 1, lists a delivery date but the signature is unreadable. Build one short topic to learn who accepted it.” The right result draws a distinction between the witness’s first-hand knowledge and what the receipt shows. It must not invent the signer or treat the receipt as authenticated.

## Use it for a draft you can review

1. **Give the assistant the task.** Paste the prompt. Tell it the forum, type of witness, examination purpose, permitted scope and time. State what rule or notice language is known and what still needs verification.
2. **Give it only authorized sources.** For real work, direct it to the relevant records in your approved environment. Do not use a public chatbot to test client material. Preserve original exhibits and identify the version you want cited.
3. **Ask for the three outputs.** A fact-and-source table; a topic-by-topic outline with short questions, exhibit pins, and follow-up branches; and a short list of gaps or questions to prioritize.
4. **Read the questions aloud.** Verify every important fact and quotation against the original. Check scope, foundations, objections, privilege, capacity, exhibits and changes. Remove assumptions and split long questions. The lawyer makes the final choices.

If the AI cannot find support, ask it to put “verify” beside the question rather than guess. Do not let it contact the witness, serve or file anything, or change an evidence copy. This is drafting assistance only, provided as is.
